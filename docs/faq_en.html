Q1. What can this app do?

A1Bridgy is an app that acts as a "bridge" for conversation through face-to-face text display, handwriting, voice recognition, and translation.

It can be used in a wide range of situations — traveling abroad, talking with foreign visitors, language learning, communicating with elderly people, or conversing with those who have difficulty hearing or speaking.

Q2. How do I enter text?

A2The following input methods are available.

  • Freehand drawing (finger / Apple Pencil)
  • Voice input (speech-to-text transcription)
  • Keyboard input
  • Apple Pencil Scribble (handwriting recognition)

Q3. The microphone is not working

A3On your iPad or iPhone, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and make sure Bridgy is allowed to use the microphone.

If voice recognition is also unavailable, please also check Settings → Privacy & Security → Speech Recognition.

Q4. Translation is not working. Can you explain in detail?

A4The first time you use translation, language data must be downloaded or prepared. Please connect to the internet and try again.

Supported languages: Japanese / English / French / Spanish / Italian / Vietnamese

Translation pairs: 36 pairs

Examples: Japanese↔English, English↔French, English↔Spanish, English↔Italian, English↔Vietnamese, and more.

Translation Mode Settings

  • Manual translation: You can review the transcribed text before translating. Tap the arrow button (→) to the right of the microphone.
  • Auto-translate after recording stops: Translation into the other person's language happens automatically after recording ends.
  • Auto-translate and read aloud after recording stops: After recording ends, the text is automatically translated and then read aloud in the other person's language.

Language Settings

  • Tap the gear icon (Settings) to set the language for "Me" and "Other Person."
  • Specify the language you speak and the language the other person speaks.

Speaker Selection for Voice Input

  • When you speak: Tap Me next to the microphone button. Your language is recognized and displayed translated into the other person's language.
  • When the other person speaks: Tap Other Person next to the microphone button. Their language is recognized and displayed translated into your language.

Q5. How do I use voice input?

A5Use the microphone button to start and stop voice input.

Starting and Stopping Voice Input
  • Tap the microphone button to start voice input. Tap it again when you have completely finished speaking.
Language Display During Recording
  • While listening, the screen displays which language is being recognized — "Me" or "Partner."
Interim Text Display
  • While speaking, partially recognized text appears in a lighter color. After approximately 2.5 seconds of silence, the app determines speech has ended and confirms the text in a darker color.
Canceling Misrecognized Text
  • If there is a misrecognition while the text is still displayed in light color, long-press the microphone button to cancel the recognition result. This prevents incorrect speech from being shown to the other person.
Switching the Microphone Target
  • Double-tap the microphone button to switch the listening target from "Me" to "Partner," or from "Partner" to "Me."

Keyboard Input

  • Tap "Enter Text (Pen / Keyboard)" in the bottom menu to open a new window.
  • Select who is entering text: Me / Other Person.
  • Choose your language or the other person's language.
  • Tap the white area to bring up the keyboard.
  • Type your text and tap Add to display it on screen in the selected language. Translation will also occur according to the current translation mode.

Tip: Registering frequently used phrases in the User Dictionary (Text Replacement) is very convenient. You can display long phrases by typing a short abbreviation.

Where to find it: Settings → General → Keyboard → Text Replacement

Apple Pencil Input (Scribble)

This feature is available on iPad only. It is not available on iPhone.

  • Open Settings → Apple Pencil on your iPad.
  • Turn Apple Pencil Only OFF.
  • Turn Scribble ON.
  • Tap "Enter Text (Pen / Keyboard)" in the bottom menu.
  • Select who is entering text: Me / Other Person.
  • Write text in the white area using Apple Pencil, then tap Add.

Audio Output

  • Tap the speaker icon in the bottom menu to read the translated text aloud.

Q6. Auto-translation does not work the first time

A6Language data must be downloaded or prepared the first time. Please use the manual translation button for the very first translation. Auto-translation will work from the second time onward.

Q7. What is the difference between the eraser and the trash?

A7Their roles are as follows.

  • Eraser: Erases freehand drawn lines.
  • Trash: Clears all lines and text at once.

Q8. Can I zoom in, zoom out, or move the text on screen?

A8Yes, you can.

  • Use a two-finger pinch in / pinch out gesture to zoom in or out.
  • Drag with one finger to move the text position.

When moving text, tap the eraser first to avoid accidentally drawing lines.

Q9. Can I change the text size or line thickness displayed on screen?

A9Yes, you can.

  • Slide the slider at the bottom of the screen left or right to change the text size.
  • Tap the slider to switch to line thickness adjustment.

Q10. Do I have to agree to the terms of use?

A10Yes. If you do not agree, the app cannot be used.

Q11. Can I use the app offline?

A11Yes, you can. However, the first time you use the translation feature, an internet connection is required to download the necessary language data.

Once the language data has been prepared, the corresponding translations can be used offline.

Q12. Lines appear when I try to pinch or move text. Why does this happen?

A12Because Bridgy allows freehand drawing with your finger, the moment your finger touches the screen may be recognized as a "draw" action, causing lines to appear.

When zooming or moving text, tap the eraser icon first before performing the gesture. This makes it less likely that lines will be drawn, and zoom and move operations will work more smoothly.

Q13. The lines are hard to see

A13Display settings on your iPad or iPhone such as Smart Invert, Classic Invert, or high contrast modes may make lines difficult to see. Please try turning these settings OFF.

Where to find them: Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Invert Colors / Increase Contrast
